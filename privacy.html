Privacy · Live

Cookies, storage and your data.

Keystride is a local-first typing studio. There are no accounts, advertising cookies, analytics packages or third-party trackers.

Effective 14 November 2026 · Version 3.0.0

Cookies

Keystride does not set any cookies. It does not use advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies, and it makes no requests to third-party services.

Optional browser storage

With your permission, Keystride stores the following on this device in localStorage:

  • lesson progress, best results and completion history;
  • practice time, daily activity and current streak;
  • weak-key counts used to build practice drills;
  • settings such as reveal mode, sound and strict mode;
  • your last lesson and track so you can continue where you left off.

The storage key is keystride.progress.v1. It never leaves your browser, and no typing content is transmitted to a server.

Your choice

On first use, Keystride asks whether it may save progress on this device. You can choose Allow progress storage or Essential only. Essential-only mode keeps the app working for that visit without saving lesson progress or settings.

Your consent choice is stored locally under keystride.consent.v1. You can reopen Privacy & storage from the app footer at any time to review or withdraw it.

Deleting data

Choosing Essential only removes the optional progress record from this browser. You can also clear all progress from the Progress screen, or remove the site's storage through your browser settings. Clearing site data removes both progress and your consent choice.

Server and hosting

The app is hosted as a static site on Vercel. Vercel may keep standard access logs under its own policy; the app itself has no user database and does not send typing content to it.

Contact

Keystride is a public project. For privacy or security questions, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
